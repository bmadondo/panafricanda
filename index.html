Advancing Sustainable Development Across Africa

Pan African Development Alliance (PADA) is an African-owned and African-led development organisation advancing sustainable, inclusive and locally driven development across Africa. Through strategic partnerships, technical expertise and integrated programme implementation, we strengthen institutions, mobilise resources and deliver development solutions that improve lives.

Discover PADA

Who We Are

PADA works to strengthen development systems, build institutional capacity and promote effective partnerships between governments, development agencies, civil society, communities and the private sector.

Our multidisciplinary approach supports the design, implementation, management and oversight of development programmes that deliver measurable and sustainable outcomes.

Our Strategic Focus Areas

Social and Human Development

Health systems, education, youth development, gender equality, human rights and humanitarian response.

Economic Development and Sustainability

Agriculture, food security, climate resilience, environmental sustainability, water and development finance.

Performance, Learning and Innovation

Research, monitoring, evaluation, learning, data analytics and evidence-informed programming.

Governance and Institutional Systems

Institutional strengthening, public sector systems, organisational development and operational excellence.

What We Do

PADA provides programme management and implementation, grant and fund management, technical assistance, institutional strengthening, capacity development, policy research and development advisory services.

Building Partnerships for Impact

We welcome strategic collaborations with governments, bilateral and multilateral development agencies, donors, foundations, NGOs, civil society organisations, research institutions and private-sector partners.
